Meet Kisalan

From Cabrini-Green
to commanding the room.

Kisalan Glover is the force behind The Kisalan Experience, a platform blending entertainment, real conversation, and meaningful connection. A dynamic speaker and proven leader, he captivates audiences with wit, insight, and authenticity, hosting notable guests like Rick Party, Deon Cole, Val Warner, Nikole Hannah-Jones, and Malcolm D. Lee.

Inspired by his personal life experiences, Kisalan was moved to write KISISMS, using his journey to uplift and help guide others. In 2023, he debuted his sold-out one-man show Kis’d by Life, sharing his path from Chicago’s Old Town/Cabrini-Green neighborhood to Dean of Enrollment at City Colleges of Chicago. A graduate of Kentucky State University and Concordia University Chicago, and a proud member of Alpha Phi Alpha Fraternity, Inc., he embodies growth, leadership, and staying true to who you are.
